About the Assamese Audio Segmentation System

1. Preprocessing

Mixed to mono, resampled to 16 kHz, 80 Hz high-pass filtered, spectral noise reduction, and peak/RMS normalization.

2. Voice Activity Detection (VAD)

Framed at 25 ms with 10 ms step. Dual-criterion energy and spectral shape (centroid and flatness) classifies speech regions.

3. Acoustic Feature Extraction

Extracts short-time energy (STE), zero-crossing rate (ZCR), autocorrelation fundamental frequency Pitch (F0), and 13 MFCCs.

4. Boundary Detection

Detects sentence boundaries using pause duration thresholds reinforced by acoustic cues (pitch reset, energy drop, spectral rise).

5. Evaluation

Scored against reference boundaries using Precision, Recall, F1-Score, and R-Value (Räsänen et al., 2009).

6. Segmentation & Export

Interactive waveform & spectrogram with manual editing, sentence transcription, and ZIP/CSV export.

Expected Outcome

A functional web-based Assamese Audio Segmentation System capable of automatically identifying potential sentence boundaries in continuous Assamese speech, generating sentence-level audio segments with corresponding timestamps, and providing interactive waveform and spectrogram visualizations for reviewing and editing the detected boundaries.
